This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Lektori Libra website and our proofreading, copy editing and editing services, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It complies with the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act (Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia No. 87/2018).

For any questions about your data, contact us by the email or phone number above.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form and enquiry data: name, email address, phone number, type and language of the text, deadline and message.
  • Texts and documents you send us for proofreading, via the form or by email, including any personal data they may contain.
  • Invoicing data: name and address for individuals; company details for businesses and sole traders.
  • Correspondence you have with us by email or phone.

We do not collect special categories of personal data, unless they appear in a text you choose to send us. Such data is used only to deliver the service you ordered.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Answering enquiries and preparing quotes – steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Providing proofreading and editing services – performance of a contract.
  • Invoicing and bookkeeping – compliance with legal obligations.
  • Handling complaints and protecting our rights – legitimate interest.

We never use your data for marketing without your explicit consent, and we never sell it to third parties.

4. Confidentiality and NDAs

All texts and documents you entrust to us are treated as confidential. Only the editors and collaborators working on your project have access to them, and they are bound by confidentiality. We never publish, share or use your texts for any other purpose.

On request, we sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before receiving your document – either our standard agreement or one prepared by your company. If the NDA and this policy differ, the NDA prevails.

5. Who may receive your data

  • Editors and collaborators working on your project, to the extent needed for the job.
  • Our accountant, for the data needed for invoicing and bookkeeping.
  • Technical service providers, mainly our email provider (Google Gmail) and website host.
  • Public authorities, only where required by law.

6. International transfers

We use Google’s Gmail service for email, so data you send us – including enquiries and attachments from the contact form – may be stored on servers outside Serbia, including in the United States. Google applies standard contractual clauses and other safeguards in line with data protection law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that did not lead to an order – up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Texts sent for proofreading – until the job is finished and the complaint period has expired, and no longer than 12 months after delivery, unless agreed otherwise (for example in an NDA or for ongoing projects).
  • Invoices and accounting records – for the periods required by the Serbian Accounting Act.

After these periods, the data is deleted or permanently anonymised.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising, and does not automatically load third-party scripts.

Google Maps: the map showing our location loads from Google’s servers only when you click “Show map”. From that point Google may collect technical data (such as your IP address) and set its own cookies, in line with the Google Privacy Policy. If you don’t click, no data is sent to Google.

If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

9. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • find out whether we process your data and access it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased, where the legal conditions are met;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Send your request to prevodiocibg@gmail.com. We will reply without undue delay and within 30 days at the latest.

If you believe your data is not processed lawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade, Serbia, www.poverenik.rs.

10. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse: access is limited to people who need it, accounts are protected, and the website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with the date of the last update.
